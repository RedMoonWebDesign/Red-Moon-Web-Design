About Red Moon Web Design

I first started playing with code at the age of 14, attempting to build a website for one of my favorite bands at the time and had a blast. But shortly afterwards, I put it away, not to be touched for another 17 years. Then after realizing I had all the opportunity to learn and make this a real thing, the idea of coding for a living and building small business websites reentered my field of vision.

After studying and building skills in HTML-5, CSS-3, and various other web building technologies, I began building small websites for friends and family. Soon after a few business owners reached out to me to build them their own site.

Realizing just how few options are available to people in this community and surrounding areas I decided to get to work on this very site, thus launching Red Moon Web Design.

Many other developers will build your site, get your cash, then ghost you, leaving you with a site in need of updates, optimization, and sometimes a completely broken site.

Ever been to a website and found a broken button? That’s why, and trust me, people do notice, even if just subconsciously. I aim to be the antithesis to them. Jerad (Jerry) Rhone Founder
