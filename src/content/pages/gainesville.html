What Your Website Actually Needs To Do

I’m not going to bury you in jargon. A good small business website does a handful of things well, and most of them are simple:

  • Says what you do and where you work, right at the top
  • Gives people a phone number they can tap with one thumb
  • Shows real photos of your real work, not stock images of a handshake
  • Loads fast enough that nobody gives up and hits the back button

That’s the foundation. Everything else is a bonus, and I’ll be honest with you if I think something isn’t worth your money.
